IV. DEVICE DESCRIPTION 
 
SPINEsim v1.0 is a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) developed by MDsim S.A. to assist Spine Surgeons 
in generating a digital twin of the spine that can be used to visualize the patient’s spine data, compute key 
spine parameters and, if needed, to plan spine surgery.  It is a web application and can be used at hospital 
and/or home. 
The device includes an initial AI proposal for landmarking and labeling of the spine medical DX/CR images. 
SPINEsim v1.0 requests adjustment and validation by the spine surgeon of the proposed image analysis 
before computing the spino-pelvic parameters, the Hounsfield units, and the spine shape changes between 
supine and standup  positions. It also assists the surgeon in simulating the effect of surgical acts like 
osteotomies, interbody cages, and vertebral reduction.   
Clinical judgment and spine surgery experience are required to properly use the software to support 
decision making. 
 
V. INTENDED USE and INDICATIONS FOR USE 
1. Intended Use 
SPINEsim v1.0 is intended for assisting spine surgeons: 
• Visualizing and analyzing medical images. 
• Building a digital twin of the spine. 
• Computing spine parameters. 
• Using the digital twin and corresponding parameters to support clinical decision making. 
• Use the digital twin to plan spine surgery. 
• Comparing the simulated changes in parameters before and after various treatment options and between 
different treatment options.

The device includes the semi -automatic analysis, landmarking and labelling of spine’s anatomical bodies 
as seen in medical images, and the computation of parameters which include spino-pelvic parameters.  
Semi-automatic refers to the requested validation of the accuracy of the landmarking generated by our code 
by the user (spine surgeon). 
Clinical judgment and experience are required to properly use the software. 
1.1. Medical Conditions, Indications and Contraindications 
SPINEsim v1.0 is indicated when the patient has one of the following conditions (ICD -11 classification - 
ICD-11 for Mortality and Morbidity Statistics (who.int)):  
 
• Spinal deformities (FA70)  
o Kyphosis (FA70.0)  
o Scoliosis (FA70.1)  
o Lordosis (FA70.2)  
• Intervertebral disc degeneration (FA80)  
• Spondylolysis (FA81) 
• Spinal stenosis (FA82) 
• Spondylolisthesis (FA84)  
• Ankylosis of spinal joint (FB00)  
• Sacralisation of the last lumbar vertebra (LB73.26) 
• Lumbarisation of the first sacral vertebra (LB73.27) 
 
and that the patient condition can be improved by conservative treatment and/or a spine surgery aiming at 
correcting the spine alignment. 
  
Contraindications 
• Patient not accepting the application of SPINEsim v1.0 for planning his/her surgery. 
• Usage by non-trained clinicians. 
• Incomplete and/or non-consistent medical data used as input. 
• Medical images with inadequate formats, characteristics (e.g., field of views, pixel size, …) or artefacts. 
• Patient with contraindications for fusion surgery. 
 
1.2. Intended Patient Population 
SPINEsim v1.0 is applicable to the medical images of all patients independently of their gender, weight, 
height, BMI, ethnicity, skin type, nationality, habits (e.g. smoking). 
The age group for which SPINEsim v1.0 is to be used is limited to adults (22 years old and greater).
