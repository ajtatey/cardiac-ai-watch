* Supporting clinical diagnostics by quantitative measurements of calcified plaques
in the coronary arteries (calcium scoring), specifically Agatston and volume and
mass calcium scores
It shall be used by qualified medical professionals, experienced in examining and evaluating
cardiovascular CT images, for the purpose of obtaining diagnostic information as part of a
comprehensive diagnostic decision-making process. ct42 is a software application that can be
used as a stand-alone product or in a networked environment.
