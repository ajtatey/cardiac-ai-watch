Artificial Intelligence Renal Assessment (AIRA) is substantially equivalent to the following 
legally marketed predicate device.  
 
 
Device Description 
 
AIRA operates by using medical imaging data (CT scans) to analyze the size and shape of the 
kidney. It utilizes a deep learning algorithm to accurately estimate the renal parenchymal volume 
of the kidney.  
 
The output of the algorithm is a NumPy array that is used to generate a NIfTI file format, which is 
then used to calculate the kidney volumes. These values are then compiled into a PDF report that 
can be easily downloaded from our AIRA tool. 
 
The report provides the user with detailed information about their kidney size. Specifically, the 
report includes the volume of the left and right kidneys. In addition, volume masks are produced, 
that can be superimposed on the original DICOM gray scale image. An editor tool is also available, 
which can be used to modify the outputs where necessary and recalculate the volumes (this is 
optional). 
 
This information is compared to baseline measurements to detect any changes that may indicate 
early kidney swelling or volume loss. The tool is intended for use by medical professionals, such as 
radiologists, nephrologists, and urologists, who are trained in the interpretation of medical imaging 
data. 
 
Indications for Use 
Non-invasive processing of CT kidney images using automatic image segmentation and 
measurement of anatomical structures utilizing artificial intelligence/machine-learning 
algorithms. The system automatically calculates the renal parenchymal volume.

Assessment of Non-Clinical Performance Data 
 
The AIRA Standalone Study was a retrospective study conducted to assess the performance of 
AIRA. A total of 119 computed tomography (CT) cases were retrospectively collected for use in 
this standalone study under the approved Aramis Protocol 66017 Data Collection Protocol. The 
cases were collected from 3 US sites at distinct geographic locations. 
 
The study analyses included 113 cases. The sample was enriched to have at least 25% of cases 
with atrophied kidneys. Ground truth volume and segmentation were established by 2 
radiologists; no adjudication was required. AIRA: Version 1.0 was used to process all cases and 
provide segmentation and RPV for each kidney. Accuracy of AIRA was measured by the Dice 
       
 
similarity coefficient (DSC) and the Hausdorff distance between AIRA and ground truth 
segmentation. 
 
The mean Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC) between the AIRA mask and the larger Ground 
Truther mask was 0.9092 for the Right kidney (95% CI: 0.9032, 0.9148) and 0.9114 for the Left 
kidney (95% CI: 0.9035, 0.9179). The mean Hausdorff Distance (HD) between the AIRA mask 
and the larger Ground Truther mask was 9.9743 mm for the Right kidney (95% CI: 8.4073, 
12.5967) and 9.9395 mm for the Left kidney (95% CI: 7.8926, 13.6073). Mean 95th Percentile 
HDs (HD95s) were 3.7734 mm (95% CI: 3.5544, 4.0104) and 3.9151 mm (95% CI: 3.4522, 
4.6661) for the Right and Left kidneys, respectively.
