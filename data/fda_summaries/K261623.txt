AI Auto Doppler Summary of Testing: 
Performance testing was conducted to verify the proposed AI Cardiac Auto Doppler when operating 
without ECG signals. The same verification test methods were performed on the proposed device to 
make sure the performance was unchanged, and a separate testing  dataset was used to verify the AI 
Cardiac Auto Doppler on the proposed device when operating without ECG signals. The test methods 
and acceptance criteria were unchanged. The results demonstrated that the proposed AI Cardiac Auto 
Doppler meets the performance specifications under ECG‑absent conditions and does not affect the 
safety and effectiveness. Summary of test statistics and other results, including acceptance criteria and 
information supporting the appropriateness of the characterized performance, are provided below. The 
AI Cardiac Auto Doppler feature utilizes 3 AI algorithms, AI Doppler Spectrum Cycle Detection, 
Tissue Doppler and Doppler Trace, which are referenced below. 
Testing dataset: 
• Testing dataset representatives of standard clinical practices used to collect typical cardiac
images in clinical environments were assessed by experts for accuracy.
• AI Doppler Spectrum Cycle Detection testing dataset included 3197 recordings from 816
individuals
• Tissue Doppler testing dataset included 1570 recordings from 235 individuals.
• Doppler Trace testing dataset included 1628 recordings from 582 individuals.
Distrib
ution of testing dataset (Demographics and Ultrasound System): 
• Gender: Male and Female
• Age: Adult and Pediatric
• Ethnicity/Country: USA (several locations), Australia, France, Spain, Norway, Italy, Germany,
Thailand, Philippines, China (several locations)
• Ultrasound Console(s): Vivid E80, Vivid E95, Vivid E9, Vivid IQ, Vivid S70N , Vivid T9,
Vivid iq, Vivid Pioneer, Vivid Explorer, Vivid Advanced, Vivid Focus
• Probe(s): 6Sc, 6Vc, 3Sc, 4Sc, 4Vc, 4V, M5S, 6S, M4S, M5Sc, M5S, 12S, 9VT, 6VT, 10T
Infor
mation about clinical subgroups and confounders present in the testing dataset: 
• The algorithm performance was verified across a range of confounders: Ethnicity/Country
(USA - several locations, Australia, France, Spain, Norway, Italy, Germany, Thailand,
Philippines, China – several locations), wide range of Vivid consoles, wide range of probes.

Characterized performance: 
• The verification requirement included a step to check for a feasibility score of more than 95%,
as well as an expected accuracy threshold calculated as the mean absolute difference in
percentage for each measured parameter.
The total number of samples used for developing the AI Doppler Spectrum Cycle Detection model is 
9607 recordings from 20 unique clinical sites (not used for testing dataset). 
Information about equipment and protocols used to collect images: 
• Development dataset included images obtained with the M5Sc, M5S, 4Vc, M4S, M3S probes
on a variety of Vivid cardiovascular ultrasound systems (Vivid E80, Vivid E9, Vivid 7, Vivid
IQ, Vivid T9, and Vivid S70N)
Information about how the reference standard was derived from the dataset (i.e. the “truthing” process): 
Ground truth annotations of the development and verification datasets were obtained as follows: 
• In all Training, Validation, and Testing datasets, annotators performed manual measurements
after assessing Doppler signal quality and ECG signal quality of curated images.
• Four cardiologists performed the annotations following US ASE based annotation guidelines.
• A review panel of five clinical experts provided feedback on the annotations which were
corrected (as needed) until a consensus agreement was reached between the annotators and
reviewers.
Description of how independence of test data from training data was ensured: 
• The testing dataset is selected from independent clinical research institutions that have never
been used for training and validation (tuning), ensuring the elimination of potential data
leakage.
