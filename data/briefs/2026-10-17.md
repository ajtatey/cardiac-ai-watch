- **[P2] Residual Cardiovascular Risk Beyond Coronary Physiology: Complementary Prognostic Value of Trimethylamine N-Oxide in Patients Undergoing Coronary Computed Tomography Angiography With Computed Tomography-Derived Fractional Flow Reserve.**  
  Retrospective biobank cohort of 3,978 patients undergoing CCTA with CT-FFR found event rates similar across CT-FFR strata (10.6% vs 10.0%). Serum TMAO added incremental prognostic information for MACCE beyond clinical variables and CT-FFR over ~3 years.  
  _Supports the argument that physiology alone (FFR-CT) leaves residual risk unexplained, which fits the case for plaque-based risk assessment such as AI-QCT as a complement._  
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42885787/ (pubmed, 2026-10-16)
- **[P2] Coronary Visual Intelligence System (CorVIS) Guided Complex Percutaneous Coronary Intervention.**  
  JACC Case Reports describes three complex PCI cases (2 CTOs, 1 calcified lesion) planned with CorVIS, an AI-based 3D CCTA visualization system with plaque characterization. All procedures succeeded; the authors note prospective validation is needed.  
  _Another AI CCTA plaque-visualization entrant targeting procedural planning rather than risk stratification; early anecdotal evidence only, but worth tracking as an adjacent competitor._  
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886144/ (pubmed, 2026-10-16)
