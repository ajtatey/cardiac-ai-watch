- **[P2] A Parsimonious Machine Learning Model for Predicting Invasive Coronary Angiography Referral After Coronary Computed Tomography Angiography: Development and Multicentre External Validation.**  
  Canadian three-centre study built an XGBoost model predicting invasive angiography referral after CCTA (external AUC about 0.70, 15-variable model close to the full one). The authors say clinical use needs proof that it improves patient selection.  
  _CCTA downstream-testing prediction is an adjacent use case; modest discrimination and no tracked product involved._

## Clinical trials
- **[P2] Artificial Intelligence to Scale Early Rheumatic Heart Disease Detection (NCT07599956)**  
  Cincinnati Children's trial (n=62, start 2026-11) tests RADAR, an AI-assisted echo model, for rheumatic heart disease screening in low-resource settings. Status updated, not yet recruiting.  
  _Academic AI echo screening effort with no tracked-company tie; a signal of AI echo expanding into task-shifted screening, not a direct Cleerly issue._  
  https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07599956 (clinicaltrials, 2026-10-16)
- **[P2] Vascular Inflammation in Atherosclerosis - Whole Body Molecular PET/CT Imaging Using Folate Receptor β (NCT07877324)**  
  Turku University Hospital registered a 90-patient study of 18F-AlF-NOTA-Folate PET for whole-body vascular inflammation in CAD and recent MI, alongside coronary CTA. Started 2026-10-01, completion 2027-12.  
  _Molecular imaging of plaque inflammation is a research adjacency to CCTA plaque and pericoronary inflammation analysis; no AI product involved._  
  https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07877324 (clinicaltrials, 2026-10-16)
